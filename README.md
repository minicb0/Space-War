# 🚀 Space War: Battle for Ampelos

**Space War** is an exciting web-based shooting game developed using HTML5 and the Canvas API. You take on the role of an intergalactic explorer whose spacecraft crash-lands on the hostile alien planet *Ampelos*, inhabited by a species called the **Whitespikes**. Your mission is to survive their attacks, shoot them down, and level up while collecting power-ups to enhance your spacecraft's capabilities.

---

## 🎮 Features

- **Spaceship Movement**: Navigate your spaceship freely in all four directions using keyboard controls.
- **Alien Enemies**: Whitespikes continuously spawn and move from right to left. Collision with them destroys the spaceship.
- **Score System**: 
  - Score increases with each alien eliminated.
  - Different enemies grant varying amounts of points based on difficulty.
  - Bonus points are awarded for defeating special enemies or advancing through levels.
- **Health System**: 
  - Spaceship health decreases when colliding with enemies.
  - Collect health potions scattered around the game to restore health.
- **Powerups & Collectibles**:
  - **Diamonds**: Increase gun strength, allowing more powerful bullets.
  - **Health Potions**: Restore health.
  - **Coins**: Increase score and contribute to bonuses.
- **Gun Strength**: The gun strength improves as you level up, increasing the number of bullets fired at once.
- **Timer & Level Progression**: 
  - Survive for set amounts of time to unlock new levels.
  - Higher levels bring faster and more resilient enemies.
  - Special levels offer increased rewards and challenges.
- **Randomized Enemy Categories**: Enemies come in different categories, each with unique health and difficulty levels.
- **Bonus Powers**: Reach certain scores to unlock special powers, such as faster shooting or temporary shields.
- **Visual Feedback**: Bonus points and power-ups are highlighted to enhance the gaming experience.
- **Sound Effects & Music**: 
  - Enjoy immersive background music and sound effects for actions like shooting and collisions.
  - A toggle switch is available to mute or unmute the audio.
- **Responsive Design**: The game is fully optimized for different screen sizes, providing an engaging experience on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- **Info Panel**: A dedicated section explains the game mechanics, enemies, and power-ups.
